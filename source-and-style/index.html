Rosa Parks on a Montgomery bus, 1956

This photograph shows Rosa Parks seated near the front of a Montgomery, Alabama, bus on the day the bus boycott ended. The image documents a carefully staged moment of desegregation while also pointing back to Parks's arrest and the sustained collective action of the boycott.

The Library of Congress makes the image available as part of its historic newspaper photograph collection. It is a useful cultural object because its familiar visual story can be read alongside the archive's fuller records of Parks's long career as a civil-rights activist.

Title
Seating arrangements—Mrs. Rosa Parks, 43, woman whose arrest on December 1st, 1955, touched off a year-long bus boycott by the Negro community here, gazes out of the window from a seat far forward in the bus she boarded here December 21st, as the boycott came to an end. Mrs. Parks was arrested originally when she sat in bus forward of white passengers.
